1. Information We Collect

Describe what you collect (e.g., name, email, usage analytics, device info). Be specific about sources (directly from users, cookies, third-party tools).

2. How We Use Your Information

Examples: provide services, personalize content, improve performance, communicate updates, comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Explain cookie types (essential, analytics, preference). Add how to opt out and link to browser help pages if needed.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

State whether you share with vendors (hosting, analytics), under what safeguards, and that you don’t sell personal data.

5. Data Security

Outline technical and organizational measures (encryption in transit, least-privilege access). Avoid promising absolute security.

6. Data Retention

Describe how long you keep data and criteria for deletion or anonymization.

7. Your Rights

List rights relevant to your jurisdiction (access, correction, deletion, portability, objection). Provide a process to exercise them.

8. Contact

Email or web form for privacy requests. Include your business address if applicable.
